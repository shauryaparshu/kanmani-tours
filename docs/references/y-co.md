# Y.CO: profile

URL: https://y.co/
Type: superyacht company (built with Astro, images served from a CDN)
Analyzed: 2026-10-24 on the live homepage in a desktop browser
Role for us: structure and scale reference (hierarchy, navigation, mega-menu), not a mood reference

## Feel
Loud, confident, high-energy. Too aggressive in tone for Kanmani Tours, but excellent structure.

## Typography (measured)
- Display: GT America Compressed, uppercase, weight 400.
  - Hero heading: 160px, line-height 0.85, letter-spacing -0.02em
  - Section headings: 80px, same style
- Body and nav: GT America Standard.
  - Body: 16px, line-height 1.5, color #202020
  - Nav: 14px, weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing about 0.04em
- Paid fonts. We use free equivalents instead (see `notes.md`). Note the key idea: tight line-height and very large display type create impact.

## Color (measured)
- Accent: #FFC719 (saturated yellow), used for the enquire button, the search frame, and the logo
- Text: #202020
- Backgrounds: white sections, full-bleed dark hero video
- Corners: square (no rounding)

## Layout and structure (observed)
1. Full-bleed hero video with a dark overlay, a huge uppercase headline, and a large search bar with animated typing text.
2. "More than a yacht company" section: huge heading on the left, small paragraph below, a large image sliding in from the right as you scroll.
3. Service sections (charter, sales) with a headline, short paragraph, button, and image pair.
4. Carousels of featured items.
5. Story and article cards, newsletter sign-up, footer with link columns and office phone numbers.

## Navigation
- Hamburger plus logo plus seven text links plus a highlighted "Charter AI" link.
- Search, bookmarks, account, and a yellow Enquire button on the right.
- The logo shrinks to a single "Y" mark when you scroll.
- Mega-menus: each top-level item opens a panel with sub-links and one featured item (a spotlight card with image).
- A moodboard feature lets visitors save items (an interesting idea for tour wish-lists).

## Motion (observed)
- Animated typing text in the search bar.
- Large image slides into place beside the heading on scroll.
- Carousels.
- No animation library was found in the page's global scope. Treat as unconfirmed.

## Take
- Oversized hero type with tight line-height.
- One accent color used sparingly, but in a warmer, softer tone for us.
- Nav that shrinks its logo on scroll.
- Mega-menu with one featured item.
- Big heading plus sliding image section.
- The idea of a saved-trips moodboard.

## Avoid
- Loud yellow with hard square corners (wrong mood for us).
- All-uppercase condensed type everywhere.
- Search-first hero (we sell curated tours, not an inventory).

## Not verified
- Mobile layout (not checked).
- Measurements were taken at a very wide screen (2560px).
- The yellow was measured from an element on the page, so exact usage is approximate.
