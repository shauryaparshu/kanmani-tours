# Adams & Butler: profile

URL: https://www.adamsandbutler.com/
Type: luxury tour operator (WordPress site)
Analyzed: 2026-10-24 on the live homepage in a desktop browser
Role for us: primary mood reference (warm, editorial, quiet luxury)

## Feel
Quiet, warm, editorial. Sells trust and taste, not energy.

## Typography (measured)
- Display: Nocturne (serif), weight 300. Headings 52px, line-height about 1.2. Smaller heading 24px.
- Body: a light sans named "Gt", weight 300, 17px, line-height about 1.66.
- Also loaded: Tisa (serif, weight 300).
- Small links and nav: uppercase, widely letter-spaced sans, small size (from the screenshot).
- Paid fonts. We use a free equivalent instead (see `notes.md`).

## Color (measured)
- Page background: #FAF9F6 (warm off-white)
- Headline color: #613842 (muted burgundy)
- Alternate section background: slightly darker warm gray (from the screenshot)
- Footer: #2D2D2D
- Hero heading: white over a dark video overlay
- Accent usage: burgundy only, on the "chat with us" tab, back-to-top button, headings, and underlined links

## Layout and structure (observed)
1. Full-screen autoplay hero video with a dark overlay. Heading and three small text links with circle arrows. Trust logos along the bottom edge.
2. Intro section: tall portrait image plus a landscape image offset behind it, with a heading and short paragraphs beside them.
3. "Tailor made" section on a slightly darker background, heading left and text right.
4. Horizontal slider of tall image cards, title at bottom-left of each card, with prev/next circle buttons.
5. Destinations list, "Experience designers" section, press, testimonials, newsletter, footer with several link columns and contact numbers.

## Navigation
- Transparent over the hero with a tiny logo mark.
- Becomes a solid white bar after scrolling.
- Six top-level links, a search icon, and a wishlist heart.
- Persistent small vertical "chat with us" tab on the left edge. Circle back-to-top button.

## Motion (observed)
- Headings and images fade in as they enter the screen (one screenshot caught a heading mid-fade).
- Slider for cards.
- No animation library was found in the page's global scope (only jQuery). A bundled library can hide there, so treat this as unconfirmed.

## Copy tone
Polished, warm, a little wordy. Some paragraphs are long and dense.

## Take
- Serif display plus light sans pairing.
- Warm off-white background with one muted deep accent.
- Slow fade-in reveals.
- Overlapping offset images instead of a plain grid.
- Tall image cards with the title on the photo.
- Nav that changes state on scroll.

## Avoid
- Long dense paragraphs.
- Hero crowded with logos.
- Copying their imagery, copy, or exact layout.

## Not verified
- Mobile layout (not checked).
- Measurements were taken at a very wide screen (2560px), so sizes may be capped or fluid at other widths.
- Lower sections (testimonials, footer) are known from the page text only.
